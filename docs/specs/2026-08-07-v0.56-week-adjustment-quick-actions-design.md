# v0.56 Design: Week Adjustment Quick Actions

Date: 2026-08-07
Status: Draft (implemented in this lane)

## Problem

The planning engine already supports week-level adjustments (`reduce_load`,
`increase_load`, `skip_week`), but athletes cannot apply them directly from
Train. That makes simple week corrections slower than they need to be.

## Goal

Expose the existing week-level adjustment actions directly in the Train week
header for the currently open skeleton week.

## Scope

- Show compact week adjustment controls in Train.
- Reuse `update_training_plan` actions only.
- Target the open week's `skeletonWeek`.
- Revalidate `/train` and `/` on success.

## Non-Goals

- No new adjustment actions.
- No change to adjustment math.
- No cross-week editing.
- No schema migration.

## UX Contract

- Controls show three actions:
  - `Ease week`
  - `Boost week`
  - `Skip week`
- Actions are available only when an open week exists.
- The action is intentionally compact and placed with other planning controls.

## Technical Contract

- Action wrapper validates `weekAction` and `weekNumber`.
- Server action delegates to `update_training_plan`.
- Train passes `week.skeletonWeek` from the current materialized week.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Athlete can ease, boost, or skip the open week from Train.
- Only the open week is targeted.
- Full gate passes: format, typecheck, tests, build.

## Rollback

Revert the Train action wrapper and UI component while keeping backend week
adjustment support intact.
